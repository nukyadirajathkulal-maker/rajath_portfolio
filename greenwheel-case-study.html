01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

About GreenWheel

GreenWheel is a conceptual electric scooter rental platform designed to encourage cleaner and more convenient transportation in Udupi. As Udupi continues to attract tourists, the need for convenient local transportation may increase. I wanted to explore how electric scooters could provide an alternative to traditional petrol-powered rentals while making it easier for visitors and local residents to travel around the city. Through GreenWheel, I aim to combine technology, convenience and environmental responsibility in one digital experience.

MY VISION

Make eco-friendly transportation accessible and encourage people to choose cleaner ways to explore Udupi.

02 / WHY UDUPI?

A Growing Tourism Destination Deserves Sustainable Transportation

Udupi is known for its beautiful beaches, temples, culture and tourist attractions.

Visitors travel between places such as Malpe Beach, Sri Krishna Temple, Kaup Lighthouse and other nearby destinations.

With the potential for increased tourism, transportation choices become important. I see an opportunity to encourage electric mobility by providing tourists with an easy way to rent electric scooters.

03 / THE SOCIETAL PROBLEM

The Problem I Want to Address

As tourism and local transportation needs potentially increase, continued dependence on petrol-powered vehicles can contribute to fuel consumption, air pollution and carbon emissions. Tourists also need convenient and accessible transportation to explore different destinations.

01

Dependence on Petrol Vehicles

Petrol-powered scooters consume fossil fuels and produce exhaust emissions during use.

02

Environmental Pollution

Increased use of petrol-powered transportation can add to air pollution and greenhouse gas emissions.

03

Transportation Challenges for Tourists

Visitors may find it difficult to discover suitable, affordable and convenient rental options.

04

Limited Awareness of EV Rentals

People need better access to information about electric scooter rentals and their benefits.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

How can we reduce dependence on petrol-powered rental scooters and make sustainable transportation more accessible to tourists and local residents in Udupi?

04 / MY SOLUTION

Introducing GreenWheel

GreenWheel is my proposed solution to make electric scooter rentals easier to discover, understand and book. Instead of searching through different rental providers, users can explore available electric scooters through one simple digital platform.

  • Discover available electric scooters.
  • Compare vehicle specifications and rental prices.
  • Choose rental duration and pickup details.
  • Complete the booking through online payment.
  • Receive booking confirmation.

06 / ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT

Designing for a Cleaner Future

Electric scooters do not produce tailpipe emissions while operating. Their overall environmental impact also depends on electricity generation, battery production and the scooter's lifecycle. GreenWheel aims to encourage cleaner local travel by making electric mobility more accessible.

01

Less Petrol Dependence

Offer an alternative to petrol-powered scooters for local journeys.

02

Zero Tailpipe Emissions

Electric scooters produce no exhaust emissions during operation.

03

Cleaner Travel Choices

Encourage awareness and consideration of electric mobility.

These are intended benefits of the proposed concept, not measured environmental results.

13 / MY LEARNINGS

What I Learned from GreenWheel

GreenWheel helped me understand how a real-world societal problem can become a digital product concept.

I learned to think beyond visual design and focus on the problem, user needs and the value a digital product can bring to people.

This project improved my understanding of user flows, interface design, reusable components, typography, colour selection and prototyping using Figma.

Most importantly, it taught me that design should not only make products look attractive. It should also make everyday experiences easier and contribute positively to society.
