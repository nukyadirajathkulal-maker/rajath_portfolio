01 / OVERVIEW

About the Project

Udupi Hotel is a family-owned hotel in Bengaluru. This project focuses on designing an independent restaurant website where customers can explore food, browse the menu, add items to the cart and proceed through checkout.

02 / THE PROBLEM

Understanding the Challenge

Small restaurants often depend on third-party food delivery platforms for online discovery and orders.

  • Dependence on external ordering platforms.
  • Limited control over the direct customer experience.
  • Platform commissions and promotions can affect margins.
  • No dedicated website to explore the restaurant menu.

03 / THE SOLUTION

A Direct Ordering Experience

Design a dedicated restaurant website that gives customers a simple way to discover the hotel and explore the ordering journey.

  • Restaurant website with clear navigation.
  • Food categories and menu presentation.
  • Simple add-to-cart experience.
  • Checkout and order confirmation screens.

06 / UI DESIGN

Designed Screens

From discovering the restaurant to completing an order, I designed each screen to create a simple and convenient online food ordering experience.

01. Home Screen

Introduces the restaurant and highlights popular dishes.

02. Login Screen

Allows existing customers to securely access their accounts.

03. Sign in Screen

Provides a simple registration experience for new customers.

04. Food Menu – 01

Displays food categories and available dishes.

05. Food Menu – 02

Showcases more food options with clear pricing.

06. Food Details

Shows dish information, pricing and add-to-cart options.

07. My Cart

Allows customers to review selected dishes and order totals.

08. Payment Screen

Provides a clear payment process for placing an order.

09. Payment Success

Confirms successful payment and order completion.

07 / DESIGN DECISIONS

Designing for Convenience

Clear Visual Hierarchy

Important actions such as View Menu and Add to Cart are easy to identify.

Food-Focused Layout

Food images and readable prices help customers explore the menu.

Simple Checkout

A clear order summary helps customers review selections before ordering.
